Executive summary
Automated testing revealed that while most Maestro scenarios technically 'pass,' significant critical defects persist across the mobile app. The primary issues are not functional test failures, but widespread and severe visual/UI rendering defects that negatively impact user experience and core business logic, especially regarding advertisement placement, subscriber paywalls, content rendering, and overall layout consistency. Several scenarios (notably SC_03, SC_05, SC_13, SC_14, SC_15) failed automated checks, and even 'pass' cases often reveal high-severity UX and visual defects via screenshot AI and cross-device image comparison. Most notably, ad containers often render as blank, overlap content, or display where they should not (e.g., behind paywalls). Key areas like content loading, podcast sections, and UI alignment are repeatedly broken.

Release recommendation
Do NOT release. Although functional tests mostly pass, pervasive high-severity UI and UX defects (ads, content missing, layout corruption, paywall violations) present major risks to user experience, monetization, and subscriber trust. The current build does not meet minimum quality standards.

  • Ads visible on paywalled/subscriber screens (monetization and UX violation)
  • Advertisement containers frequently blank, missing, or overlapping key content
  • Critical app sections (podcast, article, main content) fail to load or appear stuck in loading state
  • Broken or missing UI/layout components causing unreadable or incomplete screens
  • Misaligned or cropped UI elements (cards, buttons, navigation bars, text)
  • Dynamic-content placeholders and loading skeletons remain visible
  • Reference/dynamic-content noise mostly avoided—defects identified are persistent, non-content-dependent regressions
